# 📚 BookVerse Retail Sales & Business Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

BookVerse Retail Sales & Business Intelligence is a SQL-based data analytics project developed to analyze the sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product and genre performance, and inventory position of a retail bookstore.

The project uses MySQL and a relational database consisting of three main tables:

- Books
- Customers
- Orders

The tables are connected using primary and foreign key relationships.

The main objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights that can support sales, inventory, customer engagement, and promotional decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on five major business areas:

### 1. Sales Performance
- Analyze total orders and quantity sold
- Identify revenue generated by books, authors and genres
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends

### 2. Customer Behaviour
- Identify customers with the highest number of orders
- Analyze customer spending
- Identify high-value and repeat customers

### 3. Product & Genre Performance
- Identify best-selling books
- Find top-performing genres and authors
- Identify low-sales books

### 4. Inventory Position
- Analyze current stock levels
- Identify low-stock books
- Find books with high stock but low sales

### 5. Business Opportunities
- Identify books that require restocking
- Identify products suitable for promotions
- Identify high-value customers

## 🗄️ Database Structure

The database contains three tables.

### 📚 Books

Contains book and inventory information.

- Book_ID
- Title
- Author
- Genre
- Published_Year
- Price
- Stock

### 👥 Customers

Contains customer information.

- Customer_ID
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- City
- Country

### 🛒 Orders

Contains sales transaction information.

- Order_ID
- Customer_ID
- Book_ID
- Order_Date
- Quantity
- Total_Amount

---

## 🔗 Database Relationships

The database follows a relational structure:

- One customer can place many orders.
- One book can appear in many orders.
- `Customer_ID` and `Book_ID` are used as foreign keys in the Orders table.

## 📈 Key Findings

Some important findings from the analysis include:

- **500 orders** were analyzed.
- **2,697 copies** were sold.
- Total revenue was **$75,628.66**.
- **Integrated secondary access** generated the highest book revenue at **$1,104.69**.
- **Realigned multi-tasking installation** had the highest sales volume with **28 copies sold**.
- **Romance** was the highest revenue-generating genre at approximately **$13,087**.
- **Mystery** had the highest sales volume with **504 copies sold**.
- **Kim Turner** was the highest-value customer with **$1,398.90** in purchases.
- **139 repeat customers** were identified.
- **183 books** had no recorded orders.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the SQL analysis:

### Inventory Management
Restock high-selling books before they run out.

### Promotional Strategy
Run offers on high-stock, low-sales and no-order books.

### Customer Engagement
Reward repeat and high-value customers to improve customer retention.

### Genre Strategy
Maintain sufficient inventory for popular genres.

### Sales Monitoring
Track monthly and yearly revenue and separately monitor high-volume and high-value books.
